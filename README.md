A two-line family scene for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene" bounty. Dad tells his chicken-crossing-the-road joke for the four-hundredth time at dinner and nobody laughs — then his little boy whispers, "Tell it again, Dad," and Dad's whole face lights up like he's won something. Warm, funny, heartfelt, and specific: a small domestic moment with genuine affection.

## Run it
